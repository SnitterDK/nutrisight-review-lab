GOVERNANCE FIRST

Safety is part of the product, not fine print

01

No diagnosis

The prototype does not diagnose conditions, calculate treatment, or replace clinical judgment.

02

Visible uncertainty

Missing details remain visible instead of being silently converted into false precision.

03

Human correction

People can revise the record before using it in a conversation with a qualified professional.

04

Local by default

This demo stores the current brief in the browser and has no analytics or remote database.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Less friction. Better questions. More honest AI.

Food logging is tedious, while automated nutrition claims can hide uncertainty. NutriSight Review Lab explores a safer middle ground: technology organizes the evidence, the individual corrects the context, and credentialed nutrition professionals retain the role of interpretation. The prototype seeks an RDN advisor for validation and pilot design.
